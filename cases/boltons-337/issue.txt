Non-empty `dictutils.OMD` cannot be loaded from `pickle`

A non-empty `OrderedMultiDict` can be serialized, but cannot be deserialized.
A empty `OrderedMultiDict` can be both serialized and deserialized.


* Python `3.11.3`
* Version `23.0.0`

In [1]: import pickle

In [2]: import boltons.dictutils

In [3]: pickle.loads(pickle.dumps(boltons.dictutils.OMD()))
Out[3]: OrderedMultiDict([])
